Design Rationale

Speech Bubble Plate

Combines a plate (food) with a speech bubble (recommendations/social). The fork and spoon represent dining, while the circular speech bubble shape emphasizes the conversational, friend-to-friend recommendation aspect. The warm coral-to-orange gradient evokes appetite and warmth.

Tasty Star ⭐

A playful star with a "bite" taken out of it. Represents both rating/recommendations (stars) and food (the bite). Simple, memorable, and works beautifully as an app icon. The golden-orange gradient feels warm and inviting, like comfort food. The sparkles add a touch of delight and discovery.

Usage

Both logos are provided as scalable SVG files. You can use them in your app as-is, or generate PNG versions for various icon sizes (64px, 128px, 192px, 512px) for PWA and mobile app icons.
